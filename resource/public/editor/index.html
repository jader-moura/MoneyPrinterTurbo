Narration script

The words and their order, for the whole video. Pictures are matched to them by time, so moving a picture never moves a word. Saving here does not re-record on its own — press Re-record narration when you want the new words spoken.

Effects for the whole video

The transition used wherever a scene has not chosen its own. Each scene can still override it in its own panel.

Motion is baked in when a picture is drawn, so this applies to pictures made from now on. To change one that already exists, use that scene's own motion — it re-renders the clip.

Subtitles

How the captions look in the finished video. The preview above updates as you change these, in the real font and at the real size. To fix the words, click the caption on the picture.

Caption

The words on screen at . This changes the caption only — the voice and the timing stay exactly as they are. To change what is spoken, use Script and re-record; that regenerates the captions and will discard edits made here.

Sound

Plays under the whole video. The volume is relative to the narration — 0.2 sits well behind a voice. Changing it here affects only this video, not your other tasks.

Sound on every cut

Played each time the video cuts to the next picture. Choose None to take it off the whole video. A scene can still override this one cut at a time, in its own panel.

YouTube

Nothing here goes into the video. It is the text and the picture you paste into YouTube when you upload, and it is saved with this video so you can come back to it.
